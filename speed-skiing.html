Speed skiing

Speed skiing is the fastest non-motorised sport on earth: a straight line, long skis, and gravity. I first raced in 2011, raced for Great Britain on the FIS World Cup until retiring in 2021, and returned to the circuit in the 2024/25 season.

Record

  • FIS Speed Ski World Cup champion, 2014, Speed 2 category, raced on downhill skis.
  • Personal best: 231.66 km/h, set at the Speed Masters, Vars, France, 2015. The fastest by a Briton since 2005, and the third fastest Briton ever.
  • 210.035 km/h on downhill skis, Vars, 2014: third in the category’s history.
  • World indoor speed skiing record: 104.956 km/h, set at SnowHall Amnéville, France, 18 May 2015.
  • Best world ranking in S1, the top category: 5th, in the 2018 and 2020 seasons.
  • World Cup podium at Sun Peaks, Canada, 6 March 2018.
  • Crashed at 216.56 km/h at Vars on 21 March 2016. Returned to racing beyond 200 km/h.
  • 27th, FIS Speed Ski World Championships, Vars, 2025.
  • First speed skier appointed to the FIS Athletes’ Commission.
